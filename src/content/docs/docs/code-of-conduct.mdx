## Culture

- **Builders first:** we value doing over talking.
- **Dream big, ship small:** big visions, built step by step.
- **Lift others:** help, mentor, share.
- **Open and honest:** share failures, credit others.
- **Mission over ego:** better, cleaner, safer American communities.

## Conduct

1. Respect everyone. No harassment or discrimination.
2. Safety first: people, property, airspace and waterways.
3. Follow the law and OASA security rules.
4. Protect young people. Membership is 18+ during the founding phase; see the [planned youth rules](/docs/membership/#youth-members-15-to-17-planned).
5. Keep member and contract information confidential.

## Resolving disagreements

1. Talk directly.
2. Ask the project or chapter lead to help.
3. Escalate to the founder (to the relevant council once councils exist).
4. For technical disagreements, decide with data, tests or a decision record.

## Enforcement

| Level | Example | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | A rude comment | Private reminder |
| Moderate | Repeated issues | Warning, temporary access limits |
| Serious | Harassment, a safety or security violation | Suspension pending review |
| Severe | Threats, harming youth, leaking contract data | Immediate removal, and a report to authorities if required |

## Fair process

The member hears the concern and can respond. A small review panel decides, not a single person, except for immediate safety. Appeals go to the founder for now, and to the Core council once it exists.
