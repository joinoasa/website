OASA looks for raw skill, talent and ambition. Membership is free.

## Joining

1. **Apply or get invited.** Any member can invite anyone, including whole teams.
2. **Review.** We look at what you've built or done. Raw ability counts without a formal portfolio.
3. **Interview.** A conversation about skill, talent, ambition, initiative and collaboration.
4. **30-day trial.** Work on an OASA project, or your own project if it is meaningful toward autonomy. You can ask the whole community for help.
5. **Decision.** Accepted, or feedback and an invitation to reapply.
6. **Onboarding.** Accounts, the contributor agreement and an onboarding buddy. Start with [New member start](/docs/new-members/).

Invitees still interview and do the trial. Teams can apply together and do a team trial. In the founding cohort, the founder handles every step.

## Tiers

| Tier | How you get there | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Member | Accepted | Member projects, chapters, Academy |
| Builder | Meaningful contributions over a few months | Apply to restricted projects, propose projects |
| Lead | Trusted to run a project | Lead projects or chapters |
| Core | Long record of leadership | Steer OASA's direction |

Any member can invite anyone, including whole teams.

A tier isn't a job. To take one on, such as moderator, researcher or project manager, see [Open roles](/roles/).

## Promotions

| Promotion | Criteria | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Member to Builder | Meaningful contributions over about 3 to 6 months | Project leads they worked with |
| Builder to Lead | Mentoring and judgment; proposes or is asked to lead | Core council (the founder early on) |
| Lead to Core | Long record of leadership and reliability | Core council vote |

## Age

Membership is open to adults (18 or older) for now. Youth membership (15 to 17) is planned and will open later, once the safeguards below are in place. Contract work is 18+.

## Youth members (15 to 17): planned

Not open yet. This is the policy we plan to follow when it opens:

- A parent or guardian consents.
- No private one-to-one chats between an adult and a minor. Use group channels, or have a second adult present.
- Tools and testing are supervised.
- Youth members join open member projects only.
- Public profiles show a handle only: no photo, no location.

## Your application data

Declined and withdrawn applications are deleted 12 months after the decision. For members, the application is kept while you are a member and deleted within 90 days of your leaving. You can ask for deletion at any time. The full list is on the [privacy page](/privacy/#applications).

## Identity checks

Members are verified by a video interview. Contract work also requires verified ID and any contract requirements.

## Inactivity and leaving

- After 6 months without activity, a member is marked inactive and restricted access pauses. Coming back is easy.
- A member who leaves keeps their credit. Access is removed the same day.
- Removal for cause follows the [code of conduct](/docs/code-of-conduct/).
