## Default licenses

| Work | License |
|---|---|
| Software | Apache 2.0 |
| Hardware | CERN-OHL |
| Docs and courses | CC BY-SA |
| Upstream projects (PX4, ArduPilot) | Match the upstream license |

## Who owns what

| Work | Owner | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Open projects | Contributors keep copyright | Licensed openly |
| Standards | Stewarded by the nonprofit | Free to implement |
| Private personal projects | The member | The member's choice |
| Commissioned designs | Per contract | Private, or open later per the funder's choice |
| Contract work | Per contract | Private |
| OASA name and logo | The nonprofit | Trademark and brand rules |

## Contributor agreement

- Open contributions are licensed openly.
- The nonprofit and the company may use them.
- You confirm the work is yours to give. Check any employer agreements first.

## How work moves

- Open work can go to the company, like anyone else's.
- Company work can go open, by choice or after a delay.
- Private member work goes to the company only with an agreement. The member is paid and credited.

## Patents

Open work is never patented. The company may patent its proprietary inventions case by case.
