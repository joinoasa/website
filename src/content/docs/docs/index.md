The Open Autonomous Systems Alliance (OASA) is a member-built community designing open, encrypted autonomous systems for every domain: air, lighter-than-air and wind, ground, surface water and underwater.

**Purpose:** build autonomous systems that make American communities, and the nation as a whole, better, cleaner and safer.

- **Membership:** [how to join](/docs/membership/), member tiers and [how projects work](/docs/projects/).
