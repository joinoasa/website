A buddy is a trusted Member or above who guides one new person through their trial and onboarding. You're their first contact for everything: tools, email, where docs live, who to ask. You don't need to know everything, just who to ask.

## Before the first session

- [ ] You're named as their buddy on their trial checklist (an admin does this).
- [ ] Book a 60-minute call (Discord voice or phone) in their first week.
- [ ] Check with the admin that their GitHub invite, Discord role and OASA email are ready, and that their email login link has been sent.

## The first session (about 60 minutes)

Go through the [setup checklist](/docs/setup-checklist/) together, with them driving and you watching.

1. **Accounts (10 min):** 2FA on GitHub and Discord, GitHub invite accepted, membership public.
2. **Email (20 min):** Send mail as, the OASA label and never-spam filter, the signature, the avatar. They send you a test email from a different address.
3. **Files and tools (10 min):** they sign in to the shared drive with GitHub and open *00 Start Here*. Show them the [Member hub](/members/).
4. **How work flows (15 min):** walk through [How work gets done](/docs/how-work-gets-done/). If they don't write code, use [Start without code](/docs/start-without-code/). Agree a first task.
5. **Wrap up (5 min):** when their weekly check-in is, and how to reach you.

## After the session

- [ ] Tick the buddy items on their trial checklist issue (in the admin repo, or tell an admin).
- [ ] Check in by message after a few days: are they stuck on anything?
- [ ] Two weeks after they're accepted, a short check-in, and tick it on their onboarding checklist.

## When to hand off

- **Access problems** (GitHub, email, drive sign-in): an admin.
- **What to work on, or technical questions:** their mentor or the project lead.
- **Conduct concerns:** follow the [code of conduct](/docs/code-of-conduct/).

## Do and don't

- **Do** let them drive. They learn by doing it themselves.
- **Do** write down anything confusing, and fix the doc or tell an admin.
- **Don't** handle passwords or logins for them. Their email login comes only through the one-time 1Password link.
- **Don't** share anything from their application.
