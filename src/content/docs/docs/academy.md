The Academy is designed to teach the foundations and disciplines behind every OASA system. Courses are being written, and members can lead them. Building Academy material counts as project work.

## Foundations

- **Physics:** forces, energy, motion, waves
- **Electronics:** circuits, power, soldering, microcontrollers
- **Programming:** Python, C/C++, Linux, ROS 2
- **Math for robotics:** geometry, vectors, control basics
- **Safety:** batteries, tools, flight and vehicle testing

## Disciplines by domain

| Domain | Discipline | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Air | Aerospace engineering | Aerodynamics, lift and drag, propulsion, stability, airframe design |
| Ground | Mechanical engineering | Drivetrains, suspension, tracks, legged motion, materials |
| Water and underwater | Marine and naval engineering | Buoyancy, hydrodynamics, hull design, waterproofing, pressure |
| Lighter-than-air and wind | Aerostatics and kites | Lift gas, envelopes, tethers, wind loads |
| Comms | RF engineering | Antennas, radios, propagation, beamforming, SDR |
| Power | Electrical and energy | Batteries, BMS, solar, charging |
| Sensing | Sensors and signal processing | Cameras, thermal, LiDAR, radar, acoustic, sensor fusion |
| Autonomy | Controls and AI | Flight controllers, navigation, computer vision, edge AI |
| Systems | Systems integration | Mesh networking, TAK, fleet operations, mapping |

## Path

Foundations → Discipline → Hands-on build workshop → Project work.

Skill badges earned here can support a promotion. See [how membership works](/docs/membership/).

## Who runs it

The Academy council is responsible for courses, training, badges and school outreach. See [governance](/docs/governance/).
