OASA starts founder-led and hands decisions to member councils as it grows.

## Phases

| Phase | Trigger | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Founder-led | Now | The founder, with member input |
| 2. Advisory councils | About 25 active members | Councils advise; the founder has final say |
| 3. Council-led | About 100 members and multiple chapters | Councils decide in their areas; the founder chairs Core and sets vision |

## Founding documents

- **Charter:** purpose, principles and how decisions are made.
- **Bylaws:** councils, voting, terms and amendments. The nonprofit needs bylaws and a board anyway.

Both are being drafted.

## Councils

Each council has 3 to 7 people, drawn from Lead and Core members, with 1-year terms.

| Council | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| Core | Direction, official projects, moonshots, Lead and Core promotions |
| Technical | Standards, tech tree, architecture |
| Security | Red team, security reviews, incident response, internal security |
| Chapters | Chapter rules, new chapters, makerspace partnerships |
| Academy | Courses, training, badges, school outreach |

## Decisions

| Size | Process |
|---|---|
| Small | The project lead decides |
| Medium | A council or lead decides, and posts it publicly |
| Large (new standard, moonshot, rule change) | OASA Proposal |
| Foundational (charter, core principles) | Proposal plus two-thirds of Core |

## OASA Proposals

1. A Builder or above writes the proposal.
2. It stays open for discussion for about two weeks.
3. The council votes by simple majority.
4. The decision and the reasons are published.

## Founder veto

In later phases the founder keeps a veto on changes to the core [principles](/docs/principles/) (encrypted always, decentralized, open by default and the rest), so the mission can't drift.

## Board and company

- The **nonprofit board** holds legal and financial responsibility. It stays small.
- The **company** (OASA Engineering) has separate ownership and leadership.
- A **conflict-of-interest policy** applies to anyone in both, and major agreements are disclosed.

:::note
The member thresholds, council sizes and terms, and the veto scope are defaults. We will revisit them as we learn.
:::
